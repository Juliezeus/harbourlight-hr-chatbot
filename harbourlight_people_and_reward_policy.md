## GOV02 Scope and policy interpretation

This policy covers Australia-based permanent full-time employees, permanent part-time employees and fixed-term employees employed directly by Harbourlight. Fixed-term employees receive the same provisions as permanent employees during their contract unless a section expressly states otherwise. Casual employees, contractors, agency workers and employees based outside Australia are excluded from the entitlements in this document.

A full-time employee works 38 ordinary hours a week. Full-time equivalent (FTE) equals contracted weekly ordinary hours divided by 38. Part-time provisions are adjusted only where a section says they are proportional to FTE. A day of leave means the ordinary hours the employee was scheduled to work that day; leave balances are maintained in hours.

Continuous service starts on the employee's original employment start date and is not reset by a change between part-time and full-time employment. Unpaid leave does not break continuous service, but does not count toward the 12-month service requirement for paid parental leave. Probation does not remove eligibility unless a section expressly says so.

An employee's contract may provide a different base salary, bonus target or express enhanced benefit. Where an express contract term conflicts with this policy, People and Culture must confirm which provision applies before an individual answer is given. Do not automatically choose the more generous provision. The explicit business unit overrides in BU02 to BU05 take precedence over the named company defaults under BU01. Other apparent inconsistencies must be referred to People and Culture; managers cannot decide the interpretation themselves.

All money amounts are Australian dollars. Monetary examples are gross amounts before any deductions. This policy does not define tax treatment or retirement contribution obligations.

## GOV03 Responsibilities and access

Employees must give accurate information, submit requests in PeopleHub and attach evidence where required. People leaders assess operational impact and apply the approval rules in the relevant section. People and Culture confirms eligibility exceptions and policy interpretations. Payroll implements approved payments and balance corrections.

All employees may read this policy, including its manager approval guidance. Individual salaries, performance ratings, medical evidence, leave records and bonus awards are held in restricted systems and are not part of this source. Reading this policy does not give a people leader access to those records.

No individual employee balances, contracts, job grades or compensation records are supplied with this document. An answer about a person's actual balance, payment or award requires the relevant authorised record or referral to the responsible team.

## LEA01 Annual leave

Covered employees accrue annual leave at 152 hours per year at 1.0 FTE, proportionally for part-time work. Accrual occurs evenly over paid service. Annual leave accrues during paid annual, personal and parental leave; it does not accrue during unpaid leave. Unused annual leave carries forward without an automatic expiry under this synthetic policy.

Employees should request annual leave at least four calendar weeks before it starts. The people leader approves or declines the dates based on coverage, overlapping requests and business commitments, and records the reason for a decline. Eligibility and a positive balance do not guarantee approval of particular dates. Requests with shorter notice may be approved by the people leader when coverage permits.

Annual leave cannot be taken in advance of accrual without joint approval from the people leader and People and Culture. An employee must not assume a negative balance is allowed. Requests for more than four consecutive weeks require both approvals even when sufficient leave has accrued.

When ordinary hours change, the existing balance in hours stays unchanged and future accrual follows the new FTE from its effective date. The number of days represented by that balance may change with the employee's work pattern.

Example: At 0.6 FTE, annual accrual is 91.2 hours for a full year of paid service. A scheduled 7.6-hour day uses 7.6 hours of leave. This example does not establish any employee's current balance.

## LEA02 Personal and carer leave

Covered employees accrue 76 hours of paid personal and carer leave per year at 1.0 FTE, proportional to FTE. Both uses draw from one shared balance. Accrual occurs evenly during paid service and pauses during unpaid leave. Unused hours carry forward.

Personal leave may be used when illness or injury prevents the employee from working. Carer leave may be used to care for an immediate family or household member affected by illness, injury or an unexpected emergency. Immediate family includes a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or grandchild, including equivalent relationships through the employee's partner.

Employees must notify their people leader as soon as reasonably possible, preferably before their scheduled start, and record the absence in PeopleHub when able. Medical certification or a statutory declaration is required for absences of two or more consecutive scheduled working days. People and Culture may request evidence for shorter absences where a documented attendance concern exists.

If the paid balance is insufficient, contact People and Culture to determine available unpaid leave or another appropriate arrangement. This document does not specify an additional unpaid carer leave entitlement. Employees do not need to disclose a diagnosis to their people leader; evidence is submitted through the restricted PeopleHub channel.

## LEA03 Company paid parental leave

An employee must complete 12 months of qualifying service before the first day of company paid parental leave. Unpaid leave periods are excluded when calculating qualifying service. Both permanent and fixed-term covered employees can qualify, but company payments stop at the end of a fixed-term contract unless that contract is extended.

An eligible primary carer receives 12 weeks of company paid parental leave at their contracted ordinary weekly hours and base salary immediately before the leave begins. A primary carer is the person taking the main day-to-day responsibility for a new child's care. An eligible secondary carer receives two weeks on the same pay basis. Bonus, overtime and allowances are excluded from parental leave pay.

These week-based entitlements are not reduced again by FTE: a part-time employee receives the stated number of weeks at their own part-time base pay. Each employee may claim one carer category for a birth or adoption event. Multiple births do not multiply the entitlement. If both parents work at Harbourlight, each is assessed separately, and only one can claim the primary carer category for the same event.

Paid parental leave is taken as one continuous block within 12 months of the birth or placement. Half-pay arrangements and split blocks are not offered under this policy. Submit the request at least 10 calendar weeks before the proposed start, with expected birth or placement evidence and a carer declaration. Where timing cannot reasonably be predicted, notify People and Culture as soon as practicable; shorter notice alone does not remove eligibility.

People and Culture confirms eligibility; the people leader records the handover plan. Annual leave may be requested immediately before or after parental leave under LEA01. It does not increase the parental leave allocation. Government payments, unpaid parental leave rights and interactions with those arrangements are outside this dataset and require People and Culture advice.

## LEA04 Compassionate leave and unpaid personal leave

Covered employees receive two paid scheduled working days of compassionate leave per event involving the death or life-threatening illness or injury of an immediate family or household member. The definition of immediate family in LEA02 applies. The allocation is per event, is not reduced by FTE, does not accumulate and is paid for scheduled ordinary hours only.

Notify the people leader as soon as practicable. Reasonable evidence may be requested by People and Culture. Additional time may be requested as annual leave or unpaid personal leave.

Discretionary unpaid personal leave of up to four weeks requires approval from the people leader and People and Culture before it begins. More than four weeks also requires the relevant executive's approval. Approval depends on the reason, duration and operational coverage. During this unpaid leave, annual and personal leave do not accrue, and the period is excluded from bonus eligible service under REW02.

## BEN01 Wellbeing reimbursement

Covered employees can claim up to $600 per calendar year at 1.0 FTE for approved wellbeing expenses. The annual limit is proportional to FTE and covered employment during that year. For each calendar month, the available allocation is $50 multiplied by that month's average daily FTE, counting days outside covered employment as zero. Sum the monthly allocations and round the annual total to cents.

Eligible expenses are gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation app subscriptions and ergonomic home working accessories. Travel, meals, clothing, cosmetic treatments and expenses already reimbursed elsewhere are excluded. Unused allocation expires at year-end and cannot be paid as cash.

Submit an itemised receipt through PeopleHub within 60 calendar days of purchase and no later than 15 January of the following year. Expenses count against the year of purchase. The people leader confirms the expense category and Finance validates the receipt and limit. Reimbursements cannot exceed the allocation earned to the claim date; later accrual does not automatically reopen a closed claim.

Example: An employee at 0.5 FTE for the whole calendar year has a $300 annual limit. An employee at 1.0 FTE from 1 July through 31 December, with no covered employment earlier that year, has a $300 limit.

## BEN02 Learning and employee assistance

Covered employees have a learning reimbursement budget of $1,500 per financial year, running from 1 July to 30 June. This limit is not proportional to FTE or start date. It covers job-related courses, professional memberships and certification exam fees. Travel and accommodation are excluded from this budget and require a separate travel policy not supplied here.

The people leader must approve the learning activity in writing before purchase. Claims require proof of payment and must be lodged within 30 calendar days of payment and by 15 July following the financial year of purchase. Unused budget does not carry forward. If an employee resigns after an approved reimbursement, this policy does not require repayment. Mandatory training is funded separately and does not reduce the learning budget.

Employees and their immediate family may access six confidential employee assistance counselling sessions per person per calendar year. This allocation is not proportional to FTE. Booking instructions are available from People and Culture; no provider or booking link is included in this dataset. Managers receive no session content or individual attendance information through this policy.

## PAY01 Base salary and payroll

Base salary is the employee's contracted ordinary pay, excluding bonuses, overtime, allowances and reimbursements. Full-time salary ranges in PAY02 are annual base salary amounts. An employee's actual part-time annual base salary equals the agreed full-time salary multiplied by FTE.

Employees are paid monthly on the 15th for that calendar month. If the 15th is not a business day, payment is made on the preceding business day. For this rule, a business day is a weekday other than a public holiday observed by the Sydney payroll office. The holiday calendar is not supplied with this source.

Approved changes received by Payroll by the fifth business day of the month are included in that month's pay. Later changes are normally processed the following month with any approved adjustment. Payroll confirms urgent corrections case by case. This document does not supply daily salary proration, overtime rates, deductions, retirement contribution rules or final-pay calculations.

Employees should report suspected errors promptly to Payroll with the pay period and issue. Never send bank account details or identity documents to a policy chatbot.

## REW01 Bonus participation and timing

The annual bonus performance year runs from 1 July to 30 June. Covered employees in G2 to G5 participate unless an express contract term requires review under GOV02. G1 employees have no standard annual bonus under this policy. Employees must start on or before 31 March within the performance year and remain employed on the payment date to qualify for payment.

The payment date is 15 September following the performance year, or the preceding business day under PAY01. Employees serving notice remain eligible if still employed on that payment date. Employees whose employment ends before payment are ineligible unless the Chief People Officer approves a written exception. An exception is discretionary and cannot be assumed.

Paid annual, personal, compassionate and company parental leave count as eligible service. Unpaid leave does not. Probation does not exclude otherwise eligible service. Bonuses are subject to the calculation and approvals in REW02 and REW03; a target is not a guaranteed award.

## REW02 Bonus calculation

For eligible employees, the indicative gross bonus is:

**Sum of daily target accruals × company factor × individual factor.**

Each daily target accrual equals the employee's actual annual base salary applicable on that eligible calendar day multiplied by the applicable bonus target percentage, divided by the total calendar days in the performance year. Actual annual base salary already reflects FTE; do not multiply by FTE again. Eligible service includes calendar days within covered employment, including weekends, except calendar days recorded as unpaid leave.

Split the calculation whenever base salary, FTE or bonus target changes. Use the number of days in the relevant performance year, including 366 where applicable. Retain full precision during calculation and round the final gross amount to the nearest cent. Payroll validates the final calculation.

The company factor ranges from 0.0 to 1.5 and is approved by the Board after year-end. A company factor of zero produces no bonus. The individual factor follows the final approved performance rating:

| Final rating | Individual factor |
|---|---|
| Does not meet expectations | 0.0 |
| Partially meets expectations | 0.5 |
| Meets expectations | 1.0 |
| Exceeds expectations | 1.2 |
| Outstanding | 1.5 |

Example: An eligible employee has $100,000 actual annual base salary and a 10% target for an entire performance year, with no unpaid leave. At a company factor of 1.1 and an individual factor of 1.2, the indicative bonus is $13,200. This is a hypothetical example, not an announced company result.

For a part-time employee with a $100,000 full-time salary at 0.6 FTE, the actual annual base salary used is $60,000. At a 10% target and both factors of 1.0 for a full eligible year, the indicative bonus is $6,000.

## REW04 Promotions and recognition

Promotion requires an approved higher-level role, evidence that the employee can meet its requirements, and approval from the people leader, People and Culture and the relevant executive. Strong performance alone does not guarantee promotion. Promotions can occur outside the annual salary review cycle.

Promotion salary is determined using the new grade's range, internal equity and budget. There is no guaranteed percentage increase. A promotion and salary change take effect on the date in the approved variation letter. A bonus target change follows that same effective date and is handled using the daily calculation in REW02. PAY01 governs payroll processing deadlines.

People leaders may nominate covered employees for a one-off recognition award of $100 to $500 for a specific contribution. People and Culture approval is required before any commitment is made. An employee may receive no more than $1,000 in recognition awards per calendar year. This cap is not proportional to FTE. Recognition awards do not change base salary or the annual bonus target and do not reduce an annual bonus.

## BU01 Business unit applicability and precedence

Harbourlight has three business units: Corporate Services (CORP), Product and Technology (TECH), and Customer Operations (OPS). Each employee has one home BU recorded in their employment record on each date. Team names, project assignments and a manager's BU do not establish an employee's home BU. A temporary project assignment does not change it; an approved transfer letter with an effective date does.

BU02 to BU05 modify only the specific defaults they name. All other company provisions remain applicable. Apply rules in this order: confirm the event date and policy version; confirm country and covered employment type; identify the home BU for that date; retrieve the company rule and any explicit BU override together. If no override is stated for that topic, use the company default. BU overrides do not extend coverage to contractors or other excluded workers. Contract conflicts still require confirmation under GOV02.

Business units are policy applicability groups, not access-permission groups. All covered employees may read all BU rules in this document. Personal employment records remain restricted under GOV03. Missing BU information should trigger a clarification only when it could change the answer. Do not infer BU from a job title or infer full-time status from BU.

## BU02 Annual leave and operational coverage

The annual leave accrual rate, carryover and balance treatment in LEA01 are identical across all BUs. Notice and date approval differ:

| Home BU at start of requested leave | Minimum requested notice | Additional date approval |
|---|---|---|
| CORP | Four calendar weeks | People leader under LEA01 |
| TECH | Four calendar weeks | People leader; release-window rule below also applies |
| OPS | Six calendar weeks | People leader and workforce planning for every request |

TECH has a release coverage window from 1 to 15 November each year, inclusive. Any annual leave overlapping that window also requires the Engineering Director's approval. This is an additional approval requirement, not a cancellation of leave entitlement.

OPS requires workforce planning to confirm shift coverage. Shorter notice may be accepted only with both the people leader's and workforce planning's approval. For CORP and TECH, LEA01's shorter-notice rule applies, with Engineering Director approval still required for overlapping release-window leave. LEA01's joint approval for leave in advance and for more than four consecutive weeks applies in every BU, in addition to the approvals above.

If a confirmed BU transfer takes effect before requested leave starts, assess the request against the destination BU. If a transfer takes effect during an already approved leave block, the approval remains valid for the whole block. If a transfer changes the applicable rules before an approved block starts, the receiving people leader must reconfirm coverage; approval is not automatically revoked.

## BU03 Wellbeing and learning budgets by business unit

These amounts replace only the limits in BEN01 and BEN02. Expense eligibility, deadlines, evidence and other conditions in those sections remain applicable.

| Home BU | Wellbeing annual limit at 1.0 FTE | Monthly wellbeing rate at 1.0 FTE | Learning budget per financial year |
|---|---|---|---|
| CORP | $600 | $50 | $1,500 |
| TECH | $840 | $70 | $2,500 |
| OPS | $720 | $60 | $1,800 |

Wellbeing accrual is calculated daily: for each covered calendar day, multiply the BU monthly rate by that day's FTE and divide by the number of calendar days in that month. Sum the daily amounts to the claim date or year-end, as applicable. This replaces BEN01's single-rate calculation where rates change. Round the cumulative allocation to cents. Receipts remain subject to the earned allocation and previous reimbursements. Do not apply the highest BU rate to the entire year after a transfer.

Learning budgets are not proportional to FTE or service. After a BU transfer, the total financial-year learning cap is the greater of the origin and destination BU caps; it is not their sum. Prior approved reimbursements and approved unpaid purchase commitments count against that cap. A later transfer cannot lower a cap already established during that financial year. The cap resets next financial year to the employee's BU on 1 July, subject to subsequent transfers.

TECH learning requests exceeding $1,500 for one activity also require the Engineering Director's approval before purchase. OPS learning activities that take place during scheduled shifts require workforce planning approval for release time; this does not increase the reimbursement cap. CORP follows BEN02's approval rule without extra BU approval.

Example: A 0.6 FTE TECH employee employed throughout the calendar year has a $504 wellbeing annual limit and a $2,500 learning cap. Learning is not reduced to $1,500 because of their FTE. Actual reimbursement still depends on eligible expenses, approval, deadlines and remaining budget.

## BU05 Transfers and questions requiring clarification

A transfer does not reset continuous service, leave balances or already-used benefit amounts. A change in scheduled hours is a separate employment change; transfer alone does not change FTE. Annual and personal leave continue under LEA01 and LEA02. Paid parental leave and compassionate leave have no BU enhancement; LEA03 and LEA04 apply across all BUs.

For a request such as "I work part-time in another business unit; what am I entitled to?", first identify the benefit or reward being asked about. Ask for current or destination BU and effective date when relevant. Ask for FTE or contracted weekly hours only for a calculation that depends on them. A learning-cap question needs BU but not FTE; a parental-leave eligibility question needs qualifying service but not BU.

For transfer calculations, use the approved BU and FTE history for the relevant period. If unavailable, explain the applicable rule and give explicitly hypothetical examples instead of claiming a personal amount. For bonus calculations, also obtain grade, actual base salary history, applicable target, eligible service and approved factors, subject to authorised access. Policy examples do not supply these personal inputs.

Example: An employee transfers from CORP to TECH on 1 January 2027. They have already used $1,200 of learning budget in the financial year beginning 1 July 2026 and have no other commitments. Their new financial-year cap is $2,500, leaving $1,300, subject to activity eligibility and approval. The transfer does not create a separate $2,500 allowance on top of their earlier spending.

Example: A TECH employee requests annual leave for 10 to 20 November. Retrieve both LEA01 and BU02: the request overlaps the release window and requires Engineering Director approval as well as the applicable standard approvals. A search returning only the annual leave accrual paragraph is insufficient to explain approval requirements.
